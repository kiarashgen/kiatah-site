05 / ROBOTIC CONSTRUCTION LOGIC

One arm holds. The other joins.

One robot holds a timber in position; the other approaches its connection. The opposed rail cell followed a failed single-arm trial. These are proposed tools and motions, not a commissioned machine.

Saved concept geometry: a 5 mm stepped bracket and retained clamp shoe. The nominal post screws sit at Z = 260 mm, 40 mm apart; the base screws at Z = 150 mm, 70 mm apart. The upper screw position was rejected because the clamp blocks it. Fastening capacity is unverified.
